# What this pack does NOT cover

- **It reads SQL as text, not as a parsed grammar.** Statements split across unusual
  formatting, generated by a templating step, or hidden in a `DO $$ … $$` block are missed.
- **It does not know which role the application connects as.** `sql/grant-all-privileges`
  reports the grant; whether the grantee is the runtime role or a migration role used once
  is the reader's judgement, and the rule is weighted MEDIUM because of it.
- **It reads `.sql` and `.ddl` files.** Migrations expressed in Python (Alembic), Go or a
  framework DSL are invisible here — those are that runtime's business.
- **No coverage of row-level security, column privileges, extensions, or `SECURITY DEFINER`
  functions**, each of which can grant more than anything this lane reads.
- **Schema design is out of scope**: missing constraints, unencrypted PII columns and absent
  audit columns are real and are not detected.
