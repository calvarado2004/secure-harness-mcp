# Research scope

This harness supports quality and security for an evolving project. Teams wire
in requirements and validate checks, establish an acceptable MVP, then apply
those checks to subsequent work. They periodically review the project and
enhance the harness as features, deployment and risks change.

Do not frame the research as autonomous discovery on unseen repositories, or
treat project-specific rules as a failure to generalize. The repository tools
support configuration and review; the older snippet/proxy tools are distinct
from the paper's project repair controllers. Keep claims bounded to measured
checks, and preserve historical commits and captured experiment records.
